# The Classical Journey

A personal listening companion: a chronological catalogue of classical music from Gregorian chant
to the present, with a full entry for every work — what the music is, the world it came from, what
to listen for, how judgment has changed, and how to listen — plus theory stops placed where history
invented the idea.

**Live site: https://adervec.github.io/classical-journey/**

Nothing is hosted here. Every work links out to live searches on YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music,
the Internet Archive, IMSLP and Wikipedia, so no link can go stale, and the built-in player finds
a free Internet Archive recording at the moment you press play.

Your listening progress, ratings and notes stay in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless
you connect a private GitHub Gist or an auto-backup folder yourself.

This directory is generated output. The catalogue data and build scripts live outside it.
